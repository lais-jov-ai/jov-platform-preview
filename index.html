The JOV Platform

Turn your team's know-how into repeatable work.

Somewhere in your company, one person has already figured out the fastest way to get the job done with AI. Right now, that lives on their machine, and the rest of the team is still working the old way. On JOV, that head start becomes a workflow the whole team runs, inside the systems you already use. And when they leave, their know-how doesn't leave with them.

What your team can do

One platform, the whole team at its best.

Four things change in the first weeks: the work gets easier, the best way of working spreads, the knowledge stays, and IT finally has the controls.

01

Work gets easier on day one

One interface, connected to email, calendar, CRM, files and the industry software your team already runs. People ask in plain language and the work happens, without hopping between windows.

02

One person solves it, everyone has it

Anyone on the team can turn the way they work into an agent or an app and share it — so the whole team works at the level of its best person.

03

Knowledge is never lost

Everything done on the platform is kept — searchable, within each person's role and access level — building a knowledge asset the company owns. People move on; the knowledge stays.

04

IT in control

Right now, someone on the team may be pasting client data into a free AI tool that keeps that data to train its models. On JOV, the IT team controls which models can be used, what they can see, and how much gets spent.
